What is SEP

Three days in October. A year-round programme.

The Seed Empowerment Program is the flagship initiative of OneChurch International's Kingdom Impact Team. It's a hands-on training event - three days of skill tracks, business sessions, and community for creatives, entrepreneurs, and young professionals who are serious about building.

SEP runs on two tracks. The business of the skill - main-stage sessions where established entrepreneurs share how they built and run real businesses. And the skill of the business - ten hands-on tracks where you learn a practical, income-generating skill from someone who earns from it.

SEP doesn't stop in October. Monthly webinars, a career fair, a business devotional newsletter, and the SEP Hub Directory make it a year-round resource. See everything we do →

The main stage

Three days. Three conversations about building something real.

Each day opens with a main session for everyone - then skill tracks resume. Here's what's on the main stage.

Day 01 · Thursday · Keynote
Building a business that outlasts you

What happens to your business when you're no longer in the room? This keynote goes beyond profit to the deeper work of building something that endures - a business anchored in values, run with integrity, and structured to outlive its founder. We'll explore values-driven leadership: how your convictions shape your culture, your decisions, and your legacy, and why the businesses that last are built on more than ambition.

Day 02 · Friday · Session
Building a global brand from scratch

How do you take a business born in Lagos to the world? This session unpacks what it really takes to build a brand that travels - positioning, storytelling, quality standards, and the mindset shift from local hustle to global player. Practical lessons from someone who has done it, for anyone ready to think bigger than their current market.

Day 03 · Saturday · Panel
The creator economy

The private life behind the public work. Building an online platform looks exciting from the outside - but behind every post is a real person managing pressure, comparison, and the question of who they are when the camera is off. This honest panel brings creators together to talk about building and monetising a platform without losing yourself: staying rooted in faith and community, handling visibility, and creating with substance. For young people building online - or thinking about starting.

The skill tracks

Ten skill tracks. Pick the one you can earn from.

Each track runs across all three days with a working professional facilitating. You practise - you don't just watch. Choose your track when you register.

01

Social Media Ads

Run paid ads on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok that bring in real customers - not just likes.
02

Photography

Shoot portraits, products and events on a camera or phone, and edit your photos to a professional finish.
03

Public Speaking & Storytelling

Hold a room, tell your story with confidence, and communicate in a way people remember.
04

Makeup Artistry & Gele Tying

Master bridal and occasion makeup, tie gele for any head shape, and build a booking system around it.
05

Hairstyling

Learn wig making, lace frontal installation and revamping, and barbing - skills that keep chairs full.
06

Graphics Design

Create logos, flyers, social graphics and brand visuals you can deliver to paying clients from day one.
07

Video Editing

Edit short-form and long-form video - fast, clean cuts that brands and creators will pay for.
08

Candle Making & Diffusers

Make and brand candles and reed diffusers from scratch, cost them correctly, and build a line you can sell.
09

Shoe-Making & Bag-Making

Design and construct a finished pair of shoes and a bag across three days - and know how to turn the craft into a business.
10

Content Creation

Build a personal brand, create short-form video, grow an audience, and turn the platform into income.
